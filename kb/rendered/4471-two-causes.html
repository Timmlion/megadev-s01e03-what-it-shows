#4471 · two causes in one ticket

owner support desk known reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

#4471 was merged on the subject line alone, so it carries two bugs, not one. The table below counts them in the export, by tenant locale and channel, with the logic the desk already runs (src/lib/kb/causes.ts over src/lib/kb/locales.ts).

The two problems

  • parser — the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. Reports arrive through the app, by email, and through booking-v2 forms. Most of the ticket's reports are this one, and it keeps #4471.
  • hint — booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field although the tenant writes the day first. People type what the hint tells them, and the form reads it the other way round. Only the form channel, only the three tenants on booking-v3. It was counted as part of #4471 and had no ticket of its own.

Where the tickets are

Both are issues in this page's repo, Timmlion/megadev-s01e03-what-it-shows: #1 parser and #2 hint. Each ticket carries the number of reports it covers, counted the same way as the table.

Why the merge hid it

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line, and every report about a flipped date matches the same few subjects, so both bugs land in one ticket. Intake's tag does not separate them either: it is guessed from the reporter's browser, not looked up from the tenant's locale, and it disagrees with the tenant more often than not. The tenant directory is the only thing that tells the two apart.

Lessons

  • A merge rule that never reads the body cannot tell two bugs with one symptom apart.
  • Split by where the date is read (parser) and where it is typed (form hint), not by what the reporter saw.
